Thousands of posts. Did any of them move the crowd?

We tracked a year of anonymised movement across two of Manila's biggest malls and lined it up against every social post we could find. This is an honest look at whether the posts and events actually changed footfall — and mostly, they didn't. Click any post to see for yourself.

03

Why most posts are invisible here

A location panel is a sample. Day-to-day it wobbles by hundreds of shoppers on its own. An event has to clear that wobble — the noise floor — to be detectable at all.

Minimum detectable move (80% power)

A 7-day campaign must shift the cross-mall share by more than these amounts to be distinguishable from noise. Single-day effects need far more.

The one thing that did move
